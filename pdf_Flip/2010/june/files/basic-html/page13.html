                   Being born and brought up in a catholic family but especially learning to respect all the religions, I
                never thought in my wildest dreams that an encounter with Babaji would change my life in all the good
                reasons. Since young, I was in a boarding till my college days, I was bound to be naughty. The only good
                point that was with me all along was I never indulged into bad habits. Academics has never been my cup
                of tea, I am rather a sportsman. Till the tenth I studied normally as I was in a boarding. But after that
                my academic scores dropped. I didn’t know what was going wrong, nor did I know what to do. But now
                it has all changed. My academics score has been what has made me proud and those who care for me
                too.
                   My first encounter with Babaji was when I was a small boy in the sixth standard. At that point I had
                no idea about who he was, what he did, or where he was from? I remember he was at my Big Daddies
                house in his bed room taking rest. My granny and I had gone to visit Babaji. I went there in the bedroom
                and I just kept staring at him for two minutes, knowing not what to say. I couldn’t help but stare at him
                because he just looked calm and peaceful. My Big Dad asked Babaji to give me blessings and so Babaji
                did. But he also told me something which I understood pretty late. But it is better late than never. The
                words he said were in Marathi “Abhyas Kar” [ Study ].
                   My point of realization came a few years back when I was having continuous and numerous dreams
                of Babaji with me, my Big Dad and Aunty Jyoti at a temple which was a few stories high, the sun was
                high behind the temple, but it was cool, all of us were talking and I still saw that peace and calm in Babaji
                that I had seen when I was small. I never told anyone about this dream for about a year. It was white
                in colour, had a big golden point above it. There were many Gods present there of which a few were
                Shiva, Ram and Krishna. I was really confused on to which temple is there where all the Gods are
                together. It was like a big God get together with Babaji placed at the highest. In my dream I kept on asking
                my Big Dad and my Aunty Jyoti about where we are and what is it all about. Truly that place felt like
                heaven, a place where Nirvana could be attained, i.e. Eternal peace. All the memories of the dream are
                still cycling in my head and is like I had dreamt about it just a few moments ago.
                   The temple I saw was magnificent in architecture and design. It seemed almost perfectly symmetrical.
                There were people everywhere singing to Babaji’s name, lots of colours in the air, but didn’t at all seem
                disturbing nor was it distracting. It just felt so great that I didn’t want to wake up. But alas I finally woke
                up and belive me I dreamt about it daily. Every night when I would sleep I knew it would be the greatest
                experience as being with Babaji made me feel complete within in mind heart and soul.
                   Finally when I told my Big Daddy, he invited the whole family to the inauguration of Babaji’s temple
                in Sagar, Bhopal. It was there that I realized how great Babaji is. I never thought that the man I had seen
                is so great that he had so many devotees I couldn’t count. After the inauguration of the temple we waited
                there for the night and miraculously I saw Babaji again in my dream, co-incidentally he uttered the same
                two words which he had told me long back, this time I realized what it really meant.
                   Ever since that day I never thought of studies just to get educated, but as a future of me being a pillar
                not for me but for those around me. Studies was not the only thing I had to learn Babaji’s two words
                “Abhyas Kar”  didn’t only mean I had to study with books, but it meant I had to be more responsible, grow
                up and learn to care for others. This is what he truly meant to tell me a long time ago, which I failed to
                understand at that point of time. Since then I did excellent in my graduation and now as I am still studying
                I can see how others are proud of me. Babaji helped me discover my true potential which was hidden
                inside of me for a long time.
                   Truly he is the greatest of the greatest. All you have to do is believe in him and he will never let you
                down. He sets himself right inside the heart of everyone. Truly he is a  “Gulab” wonderful to look at,
                wonderful to think about and like a rose’s smell his blessings are a fragrance to all. It is a wonderful
                feeling to know Babaji is watching over you always. He is a guiding light, but it is you who have to make
                a decision of following it or no. For now and forever I will all be grateful to Babaji for all he has done to
                me, a guy who had no knowledge of who he is and what he is.
